What Collectors Say

"I'm a cat person, so of course this one got me. I've picked up cats from streets and even stopped for them in places I probably shouldn't have. There was just something about Calico's face that I loved. I knew I wanted her." — Collector, on Calico
"I had just moved into my new condo and was still figuring out what I wanted the place to feel like. This was one of the first pieces I chose for it. It's calm, a little whimsical, and it just feels good to come home to." — Collector, on Moon Dreamer
"I bought this together with Between Two Worlds. I liked the contrast between the two immediately — dark and bright, but they belong together. I didn't want to choose between them, so I took both." — Collector, on Moon Watcher
"This one has a completely different energy from Moon Watcher. It's bright, playful and impossible not to notice. The two work beautifully as a pair." — Collector, on Between Two Worlds
"I really loved this painting when I saw it. I'm artistic myself, so I appreciated the way Mary was interpreted — it's familiar, but done in such a different and beautiful way. I chose it for my mom, which makes it even more special to me." — Collector, on Mama Mary
